Lecture 7

Fourier sampling and phase estimation

What this lecture does

Lecture 5 sampled the Fourier transform of Z2n\Z_2^{n}Z2n​, and Lecture 6 made circuits a model of computation. This lecture asks: how can a circuit read an eigenphase? The quantum Fourier transform of ZN\Z_NZN​, N=2nN=2^{n}N=2n, is a circuit of n(n+1)/2n(n+1)/2n(n+1)/2 gates and ⌊n/2⌋\lfloor n/2\rfloor⌊n/2⌋ swaps (Theorem 7.2). With it, phase estimation reads an eigenphase φ\varphiφ of a gate: its nearest ttt-bit approximation with probability at least 4/π24/\pi^{2}4/π2, and φ\varphiφ itself when 2tφ2^{t}\varphi2tφ is an integer (Theorem 7.3). It is the mechanism of factoring (Lecture 8).

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • Define the QFT with its sign, prove that it is unitary, and prove the product formula.
  • Read the QFT’s circuit off the product formula, and count its gates, before and after compiling.
  • Prove that phase estimation reads the nearest ttt-bit approximation of an eigenphase with probability at least 4/π24/\pi^{2}4/π2, and compute its readings for the phase 13\tfrac1331​.
  • Show that on a superposition of eigenvectors it reads each eigenphase with its weight.

Proof. The circuit is linear, so it maps ∣0⋯0⟩⊗ψ\ket{0\cdots0}\otimes\psi∣0⋯0⟩⊗ψ to ∑kckΦk⊗uk\sum_kc_k\Phi_k\otimes u_k∑k​ck​Φk​⊗uk​. In the same way (3.5), with the uku_kuk​ as the basis of the other wires, gives the reduced state. 

The register that holds uku_kuk​ tells the branches apart, as garbage did in Example 5.2: the circuit acts as if kkk were drawn first, with probability ∣ck∣2\abs{c_k}^{2}∣ck​∣2, and then φk\varphi_kφk​ estimated. When several uku_kuk​ share one phase φ\varphiφ, their weights add up to ∥Πφψ∥2\norm{\Proj_\varphi\psi}^{2}∥Πφ​ψ∥2, with Πφ\Proj_\varphiΠφ​ the projector onto that eigenspace, whatever basis is chosen in it (Kitaev, Shen and Vyalyi, pp. 113 and 115). Summary

  • The QFT of ZN\Z_NZN​ has the characters’ sign, e+2πixy/Ne^{+2\pi ixy/N}e+2πixy/N (Definition 7.1).
  • Its circuit: n(n+1)/2n(n+1)/2n(n+1)/2 Hadamards and controlled phases, then ⌊n/2⌋\lfloor n/2\rfloor⌊n/2⌋ swaps; compiled, O(n2log⁡c(n2/δ))O(n^{2}\log^{c}(n^{2}/\delta))O(n2logc(n2/δ)) gates, cited (Theorem 7.2).
  • The main result: phase estimation reads the nearest ttt-bit approximation of an eigenphase, in the circular distance, with probability at least 4/π24/\pi^{2}4/π2, and exactly when 2tφ2^{t}\varphi2tφ is an integer (Theorem 7.3).
  • Handed on: on a superposition of eigenvectors it samples the eigenphases with their weights (Theorem 7.5); the controlled powers control UUU itself.
